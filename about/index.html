About Pratik Purohit

Technical Manager, Solution Architect and Engineering Leader.

Pratik Purohit is a Mumbai-based, hands-on technology leader with 9+ years of experience across enterprise application development, solution architecture, project delivery, cloud, DevOps, security governance and engineering leadership.

Professional Summary

Pratik designs, modernises and leads delivery for scalable enterprise applications across telecom, BFSI and fintech, pharmaceutical, SaaS, gaming and enterprise-platform environments. His experience covers multi-tier and microservices-based systems, REST and SOAP integrations, database engineering, AWS and Azure platforms, Kubernetes, CI/CD, observability, application security and performance optimisation.

He leads end-to-end technical delivery from HLD and LLD, architecture governance and technology decisions through project planning, RAID/RAG tracking, release and change management, go-live, hypercare and production readiness. His leadership responsibilities include stakeholder governance, cybersecurity remediation, VAPT and InfoSec audit coordination, BCP/DR, budgeting, resource planning and cross-functional teams of up to 18 members.

Pratik also applies AI as an engineering multiplier using Claude Code, Gemini, Hermes and LLM APIs. His approach combines prompt guardrails, PII handling, AI output validation and human accountability for architecture, security, product decisions and delivery outcomes.

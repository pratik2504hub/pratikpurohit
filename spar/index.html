Read less, listen more

Make long documents easier to consume.

Reading a lengthy PDF is not always practical. SPAR creates an audiobook-style experience for textbooks, research papers, reports, manuals, ebooks and other text-based PDF documents.

Open a file, begin playback and continue listening while commuting, exercising, working or relaxing. The interface stays focused on the document library, playback controls and listening progress.

Common questions

SPAR at a glance.

What is SPAR?
SPAR stands for Smart PDF Audio Reader. It is an Android app that converts PDF documents into natural text-to-speech audio.
Can SPAR play while another app is open?
Yes. Background playback lets listening continue while another app is in use or the screen is turned off.
Does SPAR work with Android Auto?
Yes. Android Auto support provides convenient playback controls for listening while driving.
Does SPAR collect or share user data?
The current Google Play data-safety declaration states that SPAR collects no data and shares no data with third parties.

SPAR Smart PDF Audio Reader for Android

Listen to PDFs instead of staying tied to a screen. SPAR is a PDF reader and text-to-speech app for Android that turns documents into natural audio, so students, professionals and everyday readers can learn, review and multitask hands-free.

SPAR at a glance.

What is SPAR?

SPAR, or Smart PDF Audio Reader, is an Android app that turns PDF documents into natural text-to-speech audio for hands-free listening.

Can SPAR continue playing in the background?

Yes. SPAR supports background playback so listening can continue while another app is open or the screen is off.

Does SPAR work with Android Auto?

Yes. SPAR includes Android Auto support and playback controls for listening while driving.

Does SPAR collect or share user data?

The current Google Play data-safety declaration states that SPAR collects no data and shares no data with third parties.
